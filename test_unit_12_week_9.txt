9) What will be the output of the following code?
def ascii_sum(word):
    total = 0
    for char in word:
        total += ord(char)
    return total

print(ascii_sum("abc") == ascii_sum("acb"))
a) True
b) False
c) Error: ord() cannot be used in loop
d) False, because the order of characters matters in ord()
Answer: True

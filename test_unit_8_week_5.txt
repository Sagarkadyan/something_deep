
Test for Unit 8, Week 5

1) What is the purpose of the `movies` dictionary?
a) To store a list of actors and their movie ratings.
b) To map movie titles to their corresponding genres.
c) To rank movies from best to worst.
d) To keep track of the number of times a movie has been watched.
Answer: To map movie titles to their corresponding genres.

2) How would you get the genre of the movie 'The Dark Knight'?
a) `movies['The Dark Knight']`
b) `movies.get('The Dark Knight')`
c) `movies[1]`
d) Both a and b are correct.
Answer: Both a and b are correct.

3) What is the primary advantage of using a dictionary for this task?
a) It is ordered, so movies are stored alphabetically.
b) It allows for efficient lookup of a movie's genre using its title as the key.
c) It can store duplicate movie titles.
d) It automatically sorts movies by genre.
Answer: It allows for efficient lookup of a movie's genre using its title as the key.

4) If you wanted to add a new movie, 'Inception', with the genre 'Sci-Fi', what code would you use?
a) `movies.add('Inception', 'Sci-Fi')`
b) `movies['Inception'] = 'Sci-Fi'`
c) `movies.append({'Inception': 'Sci-Fi'})`
d) `movies.update({'Inception': 'Sci-Fi'})`
Answer: `movies['Inception'] = 'Sci-Fi'`

5) What does the `birthdays` dictionary represent?
a) A mapping of names to their birth years.
b) A list of people and their ages.
c) A calendar of upcoming birthdays.
d) A mapping of names to their full birth dates (in 'Month Day' format).
Answer: A mapping of names to their full birth dates (in 'Month Day' format).

6) How would you access Albert Einstein's birthday from the `birthdays` dictionary?
a) `birthdays['Albert Einstein']`
b) `birthdays[0]`
c) `birthdays.get('Einstein')`
d) `birthdays.value(0)`
Answer: `birthdays['Albert Einstein']`

7) What would be the output of `print(birthdays['Benjamin Franklin'])`?
a) `January 17`
b) `Benjamin Franklin`
c) `March 14`
d) `KeyError`
Answer: `January 17`

8) Suppose you want to add your own birthday to the `birthdays` dictionary. If your name is 'My Name' and your birthday is 'January 1', what is the correct syntax?
a) `birthdays.add('My Name', 'January 1')`
b) `birthdays['My Name'] = 'January 1'`
c) `birthdays.append('My Name', 'January 1')`
d) `birthdays.update('My Name', 'January 1')`
Answer: `birthdays['My Name'] = 'January 1'`

9) What is a key characteristic of dictionary keys, as demonstrated in both examples?
a) They must be integers.
b) They can be duplicated.
c) They are case-insensitive.
d) They must be unique and are case-sensitive.
Answer: They must be unique and are case-sensitive.

10) If you try to access a key that does not exist in a dictionary (e.g., `movies['Toy Story']`), what will happen?
a) It will return `None`.
b) It will return an empty string.
c) It will raise a `KeyError`.
d) It will add the key to the dictionary.
Answer: It will raise a `KeyError`.
